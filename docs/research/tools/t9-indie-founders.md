## 3. Most common stack choices (2025-26, verified vs secondary)

- **Payments: Stripe is the default** for every founder with a verifiable public number. Chatbase (Stripe page), Postiz (TrustMRR "Stripe API key" verification, 14 Oct 2026), Marc Lou's ShipFast (Stripe with Lemon Squeezy alternative). Merchant-of-record tools appear mainly for one-time or digital products. Lemon Squeezy was acquired by Stripe in 2024 and its future is contested (2026 comparison blogs, unverified). Paddle is positioned for $50K+ MRR and Polar is developer-focused; fee comparisons conflict (dodopayments.com, Feb-Oct 2026, a competitor, so unverified).
- **Backend: Supabase or a single VPS.** Chatbase runs on Supabase (primary). Levels runs on a DigitalOcean VPS with PHP and SQLite (unverified). Marc Lou's ShipFast boilerplate is Next.js on Vercel (unverified).
- **AI coding: Claude Code and Cursor.** Chatbase lists Claude Code, Cursor and Codex; Kahl builds Podscan with Claude Code; Tibo uses Cursor. Postiz runs ChatGPT and Claude agents on its own product.
- **AI inference: vendor APIs, not self-hosted GPUs.** Chatbase (GPT-3 at launch, later not named), Levels (Replicate, unverified), Postiz (Whisper, ChatGPT, Claude). None of the verified founders described training their own model.
- **Analytics: product or Metabase, not GA.** Marc Lou built DataFast; Chatbase uses Metabase and Grafana; Postiz names none.
- **Automation: AI agents and MCP.** Postiz ships an MCP server and CLI; Chatbase has a Cal.com integration; the 2026 "solo stack" roundups mention Make.com (unverified).
- **Email: Resend** (ShipFast default, unverified).

## 4. Weekly time split across functions

No source I found gives percentages for how these founders split their week across build, support, marketing, sales, finance and legal. I did not invent them. What the evidence supports:
- Arvid Kahl's schedule is by day (Mon/Thu the solo show, Tue/Wed interviews, Fri everything else, weekends off) [unverified, betterlaunch.co, undated]. He runs one long weekly essay repurposed into multiple formats, so content is the largest block.
- Levels reports late starts (around 10 AM) and long uninterrupted focus blocks [unverified, AI-generated recap of Lex Fridman interview, 2024].
- Postiz's growth is driven by content production at volume (daily UGC, weekly X video) [primary, 8 Sep 2026], so marketing dominates that founder's calendar.
- Chatbase automated its go-to-market tracking, and founders with teams (Chatbase, Postiz) moved routine marketing and reporting into tooling [primary].

A reasonable inference, labeled as mine and not sourced: solo founders spend most of their time on marketing content and product, with support and finance automated (Stripe Radar and Revenue Recognition, support agents). Treat it as a hypothesis for the simulator, not a measured split.

## 5. Shared habits

1. **Revenue is public and self-verified.** TrustMRR (Stripe API key), Levels' post titles, Tibo's and Marc Lou's dashboards, Postiz's Stripe chart. Several founders publish monthly numbers even when they are unflattering (Kahl: "Stripe-verified" MRR shared once).
2. **Building in public as distribution.** Levels, Marc Lou, Kahl, Postiz and Tony Dinh all use X, newsletters or YouTube as the primary channel, mostly organic.
3. **Many small products or a portfolio.** Levels (about a dozen), Postma (about 20), Tibo (six), Marc Lou (12 to 36 by source). Revenue concentrates in one or two products.
4. **One repeatable content format.** Kahl's weekly essay; Postiz's value-first video and article; Postma's SEO galleries.
5. **Lifetime deals or cheap entry, then subscription.** Chen (Testimonial.to, $199 LTD), Keynejad (watermark paywall, price doubling), Headlime's LTD in 2020 [aggregator].
6. **Solo or near-solo teams through $1M+ ARR.** Levels, Lou, Postma, Tibo; TypingMind (possibly solo per teardown). Chatbase was the exception: its founder was the only employee until June 2023, and it had about 26 employees by the time it reached $10M ARR (Stripe page). Postiz's headcount is not stated.
7. **AI-agent and automation adoption.** Postiz credits agent-driven traffic and its MCP/CLI launch for the 2026 climb [primary, 8 Sep 2026; Atticus Li analysis, undated].
8. **Stripe for billing, Stripe Radar and Revenue Recognition for fraud and accounting** (Chatbase, primary).

## 6. Gaps and cautions

- Support, legal/incorporation, accounting outside Stripe, and sales tooling were not verified for any founder except Chatbase's Stripe-based accounting and its GTM tracker.
- Most revenue numbers are self-reported or from AI-compiled profiles (Starter Story, Latka estimates). Undated figures are flagged [unverified].
- Several founders (Damon Chen, Sabba Keynejad, Jon Yongfook's 2024 figures) have only 2020-2024 data. I kept them as dated examples.
- Official open-revenue pages (bannerbear.com/open, yespress profiles) returned 403 or were not fetched; their figures are search-snippet only.
- Danny Postma's $300K/mo and 18M-headshot numbers rest on one article that cites no source. Keep as unverified.
- Tibo's $1M MRR and Tweet Hunter sale price conflict across sources ($8M vs $10M+).
- Chatbase headcount conflicts (18, 26, 65).

## 7. Implications for the simulator

- Default stack for a simulated solo founder: Stripe, Supabase or Vercel, Claude Code or Cursor, an AI inference API, a self-built or product analytics dashboard, Resend or similar for email, and an X or newsletter channel. Support is an AI agent plus email.
- Time split should be modeled as a hypothesis; the sources above do not support a measured split.
- Revenue milestones to use for the simulator: $1K MRR in about 4 months (DataFast: 124 days, [primary, 13 Sep 2025]); $100K ARR about nine months after Testimonial.to moved to subscriptions (Chen, Indie Hackers, Sept 2021, [primary-ish]); roughly $132K MRR within 18 months for Photo AI (case-study title, [unverified]).
