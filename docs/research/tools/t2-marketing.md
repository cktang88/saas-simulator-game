# T2 Marketing and Growth: tools small startups use in 2026

Research date: 2026-10-14. Prices come from third-party aggregators unless marked as vendor-checked. Most sources disagree with each other by 2x or more, so every price here must be re-checked on the vendor's pricing page before the simulator hard-codes it. Items marked [design] are my estimates. Items marked [unverified] are vendor-only or aggregator-only claims.

## 1. Top tools by stage

### Idea / pre-revenue (no paying customers)
- **Website builder:** Carrd (single page, about $9–19/yr per sources) or Framer free/Basic (~$10/mo Basic, ~$30/mo Pro billed yearly per one guide). Framer is the default pick in 2026 roundups. Webflow has a CMS but its plans are split between site and workspace and can add up. Sources: [wearefounders.uk 2026](https://www.wearefounders.uk/7-best-no-code-website-builders-for-founders-2026/), [superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/articles/best-website-builders), [costbench Webflow vs Framer](https://www.costbench.com/compare/webflow-vs-framer/).
- **Waitlist / launch email:** Loops free tier (1,000 contacts, 4,000 sends per one review) [unverified]; Kit free tier (sources disagree on subscriber cap and whether automations are locked) [unverified]. Source: [toolradar Loops/Customer.io](https://toolradar.com/compare/customer-io-vs-loops).
- **Social posting:** Typefully (free tier, Pro ~$10/mo, Business ~$20/mo, 20% annual discount per aggregator) or Buffer free (3 channels). Source: [aiproductivity Typefully](https://aiproductivity.ai/pricing/typefully/), [napoleoncat Buffer](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/buffer-pricing/).
- **Analytics and funnels:** Usually not a separate tool yet. Founders use GA4 or a product analytics tool with a free tier [design].

### First customers (under $10k MRR)
- **Billing and affiliates:** Stripe plus Rewardful, FirstPromoter or Tolt. Rewardful and FirstPromoter both start at about $49/mo; Tolt starts at about $69/mo (August 2026 aggregator check). Rewardful's revenue cap is per plan and FirstPromoter removes it in its own marketing. Source: [costbench FirstPromoter vs Rewardful](https://costbench.com/compare/firstpromoter-vs-rewardful/), [FirstPromoter Tolt alternatives](https://firstpromoter.com/blog/best-tolt-alternatives) (vendor, [unverified]).
- **Lifecycle email:** Loops (about $49/mo Starter per one review, contact-based) or Beehiiv (free to 2,500 subscribers, Scale from about $49/mo) for newsletter-led businesses. Customer.io has no public price; you request a quote [unverified]. Source: [toolradar Customer.io](https://toolradar.com/tools/customerio/pricing), [costbench Beehiiv vs ConvertKit](https://costbench.com/compare/beehiiv-vs-convertkit).
- **Transactional email:** Resend (free tier, Pro about $20/mo for 50k per one source [unverified]), Postmark (about $15/mo for 10k, [unverified]), SendGrid (free plan replaced by a 60-day trial; Essentials from $19.95/mo for 50k per one guide). Source: [automationatlas Resend pricing](https://automationatlas.io/answers/resend-pricing-explained-2026/), [costbench Postmark vs Resend](https://costbench.com/compare/postmark-vs-resend/).
- **SEO:** Ahrefs entry about $108–129/mo; Semrush entry about $117–165/mo depending on source. Ahrefs AI tracking (Brand Radar) is a separate add-on from about $199/mo per one source [unverified]. Semrush bundles or sells AI visibility depending on source; sources conflict. Source: [costbench Semrush vs Ahrefs](https://www.costbench.com/compare/semrush-ai-vs-ahrefs-ai/). Most early founders use free tools (Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools) [design].
- **AI search visibility (GEO):** Otterly.ai Lite about $29/mo (15 tracked prompts, 6 engines per one source); Peec AI about EUR 89/mo (~$95–100); Profound listed Starter $99 and Growth $399 per month, but sources say those plans vanished from its brand page in September 2026 [unverified]; AthenaHQ about $295/mo; Scrunch about $250–300/mo. Source: [marketful AI visibility tools](https://marketful.com/ai-visibility-tools), [seranking Profound alternatives](https://seranking.com/blog/profound-alternatives/).
- **Reviews:** G2 and Capterra profiles (free listing; paid programs) [design]. Not verified with a source in this pass.

## 2. Core workflows (weekly)

1. **Publish a landing page or pricing-page edit.** Open the builder (Framer/Webflow), edit a section, add a CMS post, publish to a custom domain. Key UI: page tree, component panel, CMS collection table with fields (slug, title, body, date, status), publish button with domain selector. Data: page copy, meta title/description, OG image.
2. **Check SEO performance.** Open Search Console or Ahrefs/Semrush; filter keywords by position and clicks; review top pages and new referring domains. Key UI: query table with position, CTR and impressions; "lost keywords" report. Data: GSC impressions/clicks per URL.
3. **Track AI-search citations.** Open an AI visibility tool; view tracked prompts, share of voice vs competitors, which cited URLs ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews use. Key UI: prompt list, engine filter, citation table. Data: prompt, engine, mention Y/N, cited domain.
4. **Send a lifecycle email.** Create a campaign or flow (Loops/Customer.io/Kit/Beehiiv): pick a trigger (signup, event, date), write the template, set the audience segment, check the test send. Key UI: flow canvas, segment builder, template editor, send-time setting. Data: contact attributes, events, unsubscribe state.
5. **Review email deliverability and transactional logs.** Open Resend/Postmark logs; check bounces, spam complaints, and failed sends; re-verify DNS (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Data: message ID, status, recipient, timestamp.
6. **Schedule social posts for the week.** In Typefully/Buffer, write a thread or post, attach media, pick a time slot in a calendar; review queue. Key UI: content queue, calendar, channel selector, drafts. Data: post text, channel, scheduled time, engagement.
7. **Manage affiliates and referrals.** Open Rewardful/FirstPromoter/Tolt: approve applications, check referral conversions, set commission rules (percent, recurring vs one-time, duration), review payouts. Key UI: affiliate table with status, conversions, earnings, payout queue. Data: referral link, Stripe customer, commission amount.
8. **Review paid-ads performance.** Open Meta Ads Manager or Google Ads: check spend, CPA/ROAS by campaign and creative; pause losers, scale winners. Key UI: campaign table with breakdown by ad set and creative, date range, column customization. Data: spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, CPA.
9. **Order or review UGC/AI video creative.** In Billo/Insense/Arcads/HeyGen: pick a creator or avatar and script, submit a brief, review the draft video, approve or request a revision. Data: brief, script, creator, status, cost per asset.
10. **Run an A/B test on a landing page.** Create a variant, set traffic split, pick the goal event (signup, click), read results when significance is reached; ship the winner. Data: variant, visitors, conversions, confidence.
11. **Plan a launch.** Set a Product Hunt date, prepare assets (thumbnail, gallery, tagline), line up hunter and first commenters, monitor comments in real time. Data: launch date, tagline, gallery, live rank (rank and votes alone mislead; track signups).

## 3. Must-have features for a clone

- **Site builder:** page list, drag-and-drop sections, CMS collection with typed fields, publish with domain, SEO fields per page, version or preview.
- **Email / lifecycle:** contact table with attributes and tags, segments built from attributes and events, flow canvas with triggers and delays, template editor with variables, campaign stats (open rate, click rate, unsubscribes, revenue attributed), double opt-in toggle, suppression list.
- **Transactional:** API key list, send logs, delivery status, DNS record checker (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
- **Social scheduler:** post composer with character count, multi-channel selector, calendar and queue view, drafts, per-post engagement.
- **SEO:** keyword list with position history, GSC-style query table, backlink count, competitor overlap, content gap report.
- **AI visibility:** tracked-prompt list, engine filter (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews), share-of-voice chart, cited URL table, brand mention trend.
- **Affiliates:** application queue, approve/reject, commission rules, referral link generator, payouts list, fraud flag, Stripe sync.
- **Ads:** campaign/ad-set/ad hierarchy, budget and bid fields, breakdown table by creative, CPA/ROAS/CTR/CPM columns, learning-phase indicator, pause/scale buttons.
- **UGC:** brief form, creator marketplace grid with rating and price, asset library with approve/revise status.
- **A/B test:** variant editor, traffic split slider, goal picker, significance indicator.
- **Metrics to show:** MRR-attributed signups, CAC by channel, CPA, ROAS, email open/click/reply rate, affiliate revenue share, organic sessions, keyword positions, AI citations count, launch-day signups.

## 4. What changed with AI in 2025–2026

- **Site builders:** AI site generation (Framer, Webflow AI) produces a first draft from a prompt; the human still rewrites copy and fixes layout on mobile. Webflow now ships an AEO product and a Claude connector per one source [unverified].
- **SEO / GEO:** AI visibility trackers are a new category. Buyers track citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews. Google AI Overviews and AI Max shift attribution; tools disagree on what counts as a mention [design]. Keyword metrics still matter; AI citation tracking is noisy, sample-dependent and often reported on a few dozen prompts.
- **Email:** AI writes subject lines and drafts; deliverability still needs a human check of DNS and complaint rates.
- **Ads:** Advantage+ and PMax/AI Max automate targeting, bidding and creative combinations. Humans still pick the offer, creative angles and budget caps, and must stop spend when the algorithm overfits. Low budgets (under about $5k/mo per one B2B guide) are said to be too thin for either platform to learn [unverified].
- **UGC:** AI avatar and actor video (Arcads, HeyGen) makes ad creative cheap and fast. Failure modes: uncanny delivery, generic hooks, claims the product cannot support, and rejected ads for misleading content. Human creators still win on trust in some categories [design].
- **Social:** AI writes drafts; founders still edit for voice. Platform algorithms penalize obvious AI spam [design].
- **Product Hunt:** the platform reportedly filters manufactured launches and vote spikes more aggressively; asking for upvotes violates its rules [unverified, vendor blogs only].

## 5. Common mistakes small founders make

- **Buying a stack before traffic.** Paying for Ahrefs, Semrush, an AI visibility tool and a CDP before having ten signups. Start with free tools until there is a funnel to measure.
- **Enterprise tiers early.** Customer.io, Optimizely and VWO are priced for teams with an engineer and real traffic; founders pay quote-based prices for a single email flow.
- **Multiple sending domains and tools for one list.** Running a newsletter in Beehiiv, lifecycle in Loops and transactional in Postmark on one domain without configuring DNS for each.
- **Affiliate programs with no commission math.** Setting a 30% recurring commission without modeling payback against LTV. Per-channel pricing in Buffer makes the cost grow with each account.
- **Ad spend below the learning threshold.** Turning on Advantage+ or PMax at a few hundred dollars a month and judging the result on day three.
- **Reading AI visibility scores as revenue.** Chasing citations without checking that any AI-referred traffic converts.
- **Launch-day vanity metrics.** Chasing Product Hunt rank and ignoring signups and retention. Buying or swapping upvotes violates platform rules.
- **Multiple landing-page builders.** Keeping a Carrd page, a Framer site and a Webflow marketing site live at the same time, splitting SEO authority.
- **A/B tests on low traffic.** Calling winners after 200 visitors. Use a tool that reports significance and pick the goal event before the test starts.
