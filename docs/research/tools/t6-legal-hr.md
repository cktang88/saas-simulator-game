# T6: Legal, HR, Payroll, Hiring and Compliance tools for small startups (2026)

Research date: 2026-10-14. Most search hits were vendor-adjacent comparison blogs, so nearly every price below is [unverified] and needs a check on the vendor's pricing page. Dates are the search-result date unless a page showed its own date. Ramp's category rankings could not be retrieved (see Gaps).

## 1. Top tools by stage

**Incorporation and entity setup**
- **Stripe Atlas**: Delaware C-corp or LLC formation with an entry price around $500 [unverified] ("Stripe Atlas Free Service Overview", subger.com, undated, https://subger.com/en/sg/service/stripe-atlas). Higher tiers add registered agent service and EIN. Pre-revenue founders pick it for the bundled bank and Stripe setup.
- **Clerky, Firstbase, Doola**: named as Atlas competitors. I found no 2026 pricing for any of them. [unverified: not checked]

**Cap table and equity**
- **Carta**: pricing conflicts across sources. One says a free Launch tier covers up to 25 stakeholders and $1M raised; CostBench says there is no free tier and paid plans are quote-only (Launch/Build/Grow/Enterprise). Source: https://www.rework.com/tools/equity-cap-table/best-carta-alternatives (2026, [unverified]). Carta's own blog warns that customers report steep renewal increases (competitor claim, [unverified]).
- **Pulley**: the clearest published entry price. One 2026 source lists Startup at $1,200/yr for 25 stakeholders and Growth at $3,500/yr for 40 stakeholders. CostBench gives about $100/mo for Startup, which does not match [unverified]. Angel checks of $50K or less count as half a stakeholder. Source: https://resources.rework.com/tools/equity-cap-table/best-pulley-alternatives (2026, [unverified]).
- **AngelList**: its legacy cap table is being wound down, and AngelList steers users to AngelList Stack (CostBench lists $133 to $467/mo, [unverified]). Source: Carta's switching blog, https://carta.com/blog/switch-cap-table/ (biased, [unverified]).

**Contracts, e-signature and AI legal**
- **Spellbook**: Word-native contract drafting and review, pricing custom and per seat. Third-party estimates range from $69 to $359 per user per month [unverified]. Free 7-day trial for lawyers and legal teams. Strongest on SaaS, NDA, employment and lending contracts (bindlegal.com, 2026, https://bindlegal.com/resources/comparisons/spellbook-pricing-2026/ [unverified]).
- **Harvey**: aimed at large firms. Estimates of $1,200 to $2,000 per seat per month with roughly 20-seat minimums [unverified]. A small startup is unlikely to be its customer. Source: https://spellbook.com/learn/harvey-ai-pricing (vendor-adjacent, [unverified]).
- **Generic e-signature and LLM contract review** (e.g., general-purpose AI review on a Claude or ChatGPT plan) is the common [design] starting point before a legal-specific tool.

**Payroll and EOR**
- **Gusto**: Simple at $49/mo + $6/person; Plus at $80/mo + $12/person; Premium at $180/mo + $22/person (Gusto's own comparison page, 2026, https://gusto.com/resources/best-rippling-alternatives [unverified]). Global EOR covers about 12 countries.
- **Rippling**: quote-based, one estimate about $8/person/mo plus platform fee. Its draw is payroll plus IT provisioning.
- **Deel**: about $49/contractor/mo; EOR listed at $599/employee/mo (same sources, [unverified]).
- **Remote**: EOR from $599/employee/mo; contractor plans from $29/contractor/mo (2026, [unverified]).
- **Justworks**: PEO pricing from $79/person/mo; payroll $50/mo + $8/person; EOR add-on (2026, [unverified]).
- **Cheapest full-service US payroll**: OnPay ($49/mo + $6/person) and Patriot ($37/mo + $5/person, single state) [unverified].

**ATS and hiring**
- **Ashby**: entry about $400/mo at the smallest band, larger tiers quote-only; free tier for under-10 companies is disputed [unverified] (noon.ai, 2026). AI and analytics are often metered add-ons.
- **Workable**: Standard $299/mo monthly or $3,588/yr annual, the most transparent price of this group [unverified] (Rework comparison, 2026).
- **Greenhouse**: quote-only. Essential about $6K to $12K/yr for small teams; Advanced $15K to $35K/yr; implementation fees common [unverified] (Compono buyers guide, 2026).
- **Wellfound**: core job posting and candidate access are free; Wellfound Recruit seat about $250/mo; Recruiter tier is extra [unverified] (hireinsouth.com, 2026).
- **LinkedIn Jobs**: basic posting free; Recruiter seats $170 to $825/mo [unverified]. Premium about $29.99/mo [unverified].
- **Upwork**: free to post, freelancer fees 5 to 20% [unverified] (Upwork resources, 2026). **Contra**: claims 0% freelancer commission [unverified].

**Background checks**
- **Checkr**: about $30 per check at entry, up to $80 at the top tier, usage-based with a free trial [unverified] (costbench.com, 2026).
- **Certn**: budget option; $13.99 per check claimed, or $4.99 to $69.99/mo by a different benchmark [unverified].
- **Sterling**: enterprise-positioned, $20 to $500/mo [unverified]. Poor fit for early startups.

**Compliance automation (SOC 2 and similar)**
- **Vanta**: $10K to $80K/yr per CostBench (March 2026) [unverified]; a median of about $20K/yr is cited by one source. Strongest integrations and broadest framework support.
- **Drata**: $15K to $100K/yr [unverified]; a 2026 review puts entry around $7.5K. Automation-heavy.
- **Secureframe**: $7.5K to $50K/yr [unverified]; a $5K to $7K quote is cited for startups. Good for stacking frameworks.
- **Delve**: AI-driven setup with concierge support for SOC 2 and HIPAA. Little independent pricing. One competitor blog mentions "recent controversies" without detail, so verify before relying on it.
- Auditor fees are separate, about $15K to $50K [unverified].

**Privacy policy and DPA**
- **Termly**: free tier (one policy, 10K banner views); paid from about $10/mo (G2 and toolradar, 2026 [unverified]).
- **iubenda**: Essentials about €4.99 to €5.99/mo, Advanced about €20 to €25/mo, Ultimate about €80 to €100/mo (Capterra and consently, 2026 [unverified]). Billed per domain.
- DPA availability and sub-processor terms were not found for either vendor. [unverified: not checked]

**Trademark**
- USPTO: electronic base application $350 per class, filed only via Trademark Center since 18 January 2025 (secondary guides, 2026 [unverified]). Surcharges $100 per class for insufficient information, $200 per class for free-form IDs. Section 8 and 9 filings reported at $325 per class [unverified, single source].

**Registered agent**
- Included in Atlas and Doola bundles. Standalone agent pricing not researched.

**Stage summary [design]**
- *Pre-revenue*: Atlas or Doola, Termly free tier, a Word or Google Docs contract template, a free-tier ATS or LinkedIn posting, contractors via Upwork or Contra, Pulley or Carta free tier if under 25 holders.
- *First customers (<$10K MRR)*: Gusto or OnPay, a per-check background service, Spellbook trial if contracts are heavy, Termly paid.
- *Scaling ($10K to $100K MRR)*: Rippling or Deel/Remote for multi-country, Ashby or Workable, Vanta or Drata or Secureframe when enterprise buyers ask for SOC 2, Carta for priced rounds.

## 2. Core founder workflows (weekly)

1. **Review the cap table after a grant or SAFE conversion.** Open the holders list, add an option grant with vesting schedule and strike price, confirm the fully diluted total against the pool.
2. **Approve payroll run.** Check the pay period, hours and contractor payments, review the employer tax summary, and submit.
3. **Triage open job applications.** Filter by stage, score against the scorecard, move to a screen or reject with a template, and schedule interviews.
4. **Send a contract for e-signature.** Pick the template (MSA, NDA, contractor agreement), fill counterparty fields, route for signature, and track status.
5. **Redline an inbound contract.** Upload the counterparty's draft, flag changed clauses (liability, IP, termination), accept or reject each change, and export a redline.
6. **Check compliance tasks.** Review failing or overdue controls in the compliance dashboard, assign owners, and upload evidence (policies, screenshots).
7. **Run background check on a hire.** Select the candidate and package, collect consent, track status, and review the report before the offer.
8. **Update the privacy policy or DPA** when a new subprocessor or data category is added.
9. **Confirm contractor 1099 or EOR invoice** and approve the payment.

## 3. Must-have features for a clone

- **Cap table**: holder list, share classes, option pool, grants, vesting schedule, exercise price, 409A valuation field, pro-rata and fully diluted percentages, round modeling (pre/post money, SAFE conversion).
- **Payroll**: employee profile (SSN or tax ID, address, state), pay schedule, earnings and deductions, run preview, employer tax totals, contractor payments with 1099 flag, pay statement.
- **EOR**: country, local employment contract, monthly invoice with fee line.
- **ATS**: job board, pipeline stages, candidate card with resume, scorecard per interviewer, interview scheduling, offer letter template, disposition reasons, EEO fields.
- **Background check**: package picker, candidate consent, status timeline.
- **Compliance**: framework selector (SOC 2), control list, owner, due date, evidence upload, trust center page.
- **Contracts**: template library, merge fields, signer routing, status (sent, viewed, signed), audit trail.
- **Legal AI**: clause list, risk flag, redline view, accept/reject.
- **Trademark**: class selector, ID search, filing status.
- **Metrics and reports**: headcount, payroll cost per month, open reqs, time-to-hire, stakeholders count, pool remaining, compliance percent complete.

## 4. What changed with AI in 2025–2026

- **Automated**: contract first-draft generation and clause flagging; AI candidate screening and resume summaries; auto-generated interview notes; compliance evidence collection via integrations (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe agents, Delve concierge); privacy policy generation.
- **Still needs a human**: counsel review of anything above a template, offer letter terms, equity grant approval and 409A, background-check adverse decisions (FCRA process), tax classification of contractors vs employees, and final sign-off on compliance evidence.
- **Failure modes**: AI screening that filters by proxy signals (EEO risk); clause flags missing jurisdiction-specific terms; auto-generated policy that does not match actual data flows; compliance tools marking controls "passed" based on integration connection, not actual effectiveness; stale cap table after an unrecorded SAFE.
- **Pricing signal**: Spellbook and Harvey pricing is per seat, and Harvey targets big firms [unverified].

## 5. Common founder mistakes

- **Enterprise SOC 2 too early.** Buying Vanta or Drata before a buyer asks for it can cost $10K to $20K+/yr. Start when a deal requires it [design].
- **Wrong cap table tool.** Paying for Carta when Pulley or the Carta free tier covers the stakeholder count [unverified].
- **Using a PEO when EOR fits** (or the reverse): Justworks or Rippling for a 2-person company can cost more than payroll only [design].
- **Background check overkill**: choosing Sterling for a 3-person team instead of Checkr or Certn [design].
- **Hiring via paid job boards** before testing free channels (LinkedIn, Wellfound, referrals) [design].
- **Trusting a generated privacy policy** without listing actual vendors and data flows [design].
- **Ignoring state filing deadlines** because a bundle hid them (Delaware franchise tax, registered agent renewal) [design].
