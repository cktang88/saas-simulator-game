# Customer type B: selling SaaS to mom-and-pop / local owner-operated businesses

Research date: 2026-10-16. Segment: restaurants, salons, trades (HVAC/plumbing), retail shops, clinics, gyms. Comparable vendors: Toast, Square, Jobber, ServiceTitan (early), Housecall Pro, Mindbody, Vagaro, Podium, Weave, Zenoti.

Status legend: primary = company filing, government or regulator source; secondary = reputable aggregator or law-firm commentary; [unverified] = vendor or blog claim that could not be traced to a primary dataset; [design] = simulator assumption, not a real statistic. Search tooling returned summaries rather than full pages, so many URLs are the page the figure appeared on; the "no public data found" rows are real gaps, filled with [design] values.

Honest data-quality note: public data specific to mom-and-pop SaaS go-to-market is thin. Most "benchmarks" are vendor blogs. Where two sources conflict we show both and pick a planning value in the last section.

## Planning values for the simulator

All planning values are [design] choices informed by the sources above unless noted; sources conflict and the segment has thin public data.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly logo churn | 2.5% baseline for low-ACV self-serve (range 1.5-5%); 1.0% (about 11%/yr, Toast/ServiceTitan-like) for payments-attached, hardware-installed, phone-onboarded products |
| Annual NRR | 90% (range 80-98%); 105-110% only with payments attach and upsell (Toast about 110%, ServiceTitan over 110%) |
| ACV range | $300-$3,000/yr self-serve; $1,200-$6,000 field-service and booking; $3,000-$12,000 POS+payments per location |
| Cycle days by ACV band | under $600: 0-7; $600-$2,400: 7-30; $2,400-$6,000: 21-45; above $6,000 (POS/hardware): 30-60 |
| Cold reply rate | Email 0.5-1.5% (Belkins 0.45%, Instantly 3.4% as optimistic bound); phone connect 6-10% per dial, 15-25% per prospect over repeated attempts |
| Free to paid | Freemium 2.6%; opt-in trial 15-18%; card-up-front trial 40-48% (but 2.5% trial-start); low-touch owners 8-12% |
| Share paying by card | 85% (ACH 15%, mostly trades and clinics) |
| Share wanting annual | 15% (upfront discount of 15-20% shifts to 30%) |
| Support first-response expectation | Phone 5 minutes during hours; email/chat 4 hours; after-hours emergencies (POS down) 1 hour |
| CAC payback months | Median 10; stellar 6; mediocre 18 or worse |
| Channel multipliers (0-2) | seo 1.3, content 0.8, community 1.4, product_hunt 0.1, ads_google 1.2, ads_meta 1.0, ads_linkedin 0.1, outbound_email 0.2, outbound_phone 1.1, partners/resellers 1.6, marketplace/app store 1.0, referral 1.8, events 0.9, local/door-to-door 1.2 |
| Demo show rate | 85% well-run (13-16% no-show); 65% poor; same-day booking improves by about 15 points |
| Macro shock sensitivity | High: 1.5x churn multiplier and 0.7x new-logo multiplier in recession; inflation and tariffs raise closures; payments-attached volume falls 10-20% [design] |
| Involuntary churn share | 25-35% of total churn if no dunning; 8-12% with card updater and ACH fallback |
| Churn-reason mix (sums to 1.00) | business_closed 0.22, acquired 0.03, budget_cut 0.10, switched_competitor 0.15, built_in_house 0.01, price 0.12, not_using 0.20, missing_feature 0.06, support 0.06, outgrew 0.05 |

Notes for tiering: stellar operators separate on onboarding quality, phone responsiveness, payment attach rate, dunning and referral loops; mediocre operators lose on not_using (no onboarding), price and support churn, and annual-contract disputes. Asymmetry: perfect execution still sees 1%+ monthly churn from closures alone (BLS year-one exit about 22%).
