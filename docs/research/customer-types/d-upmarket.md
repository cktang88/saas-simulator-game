## 5. Stellar vs mediocre (sequence, [design] synthesized from sources above)
| Step | Stellar | Mediocre |
|---|---|---|
| Sequence | 2-3 mid-market design partners, founder sells first 10-20 enterprise-ish deals, SSO/audit log built before asked, SOC 2 Type I then II started 9-12 months before needed | chases a Fortune 500 logo with no SOC 2, builds a one-off feature for it |
| AE hire | after repeatable motion; senior AE with startup background; OTE under about 20% of expected yr-1 ARR | big-company AE hired at $300K OTE with no playbook, leaves or misses in 6-9 months |
| SMB base | separate self-serve motion keeps running; enterprise tier is packaged | neglects SMB, "contact sales" everywhere, SMB growth stalls |
| Concentration | largest customer kept under 10-15% | one logo at 30%+, discounts to keep it |

### Transition planning values
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Prerequisites checklist | SOC 2 Type I (Type II in progress or complete), SSO (SAML), audit log, RBAC, DPA and MSA template, security whitepaper/trust page, 2-3 referenceable mid-market logos, enterprise tier priced and "contact sales", uptime/SLA doc, cyber insurance, ARR roughly $1-3M with SMB motion stable |
| Months to first upmarket deal | mid-market 3-6 after prerequisites; first true enterprise deal 9-15 months from start (6-12 month strategy per source plus first cycle 6-9 months) |
| Transition cost (dollars) | SOC 2 year one $25-80K, ongoing $10-50K; pen test $10-25K [design]; security/legal tooling and counsel $15-40K [design]; first AE OTE $260-300K plus SE about $200K OTE [design]; total about $150-400K in year one before the AE |
| Transition cost (founder hours) | 400-800 hours over 6-12 months (SOC 2 policy work 80-150, questionnaires 3-6 h each, 25-60 h per enterprise deal across committee calls, legal redlines) [design] |
| First AE | ramp 4-6 months (enterprise 7-9); first-AE failure chance 40-60% if hired before 10-20 founder-closed deals, 25% after [design from sources] |
| Premature penalties | no SOC 2: deals stall or die at security review (+4-10 weeks, 50% of enterprise opps lost there); cycle 6-12 months burns runway; AE hired too early: 3 to 9 months of $22-25K/month cost with no close; custom features: roadmap velocity -30% and SMB churn +0.5 pt/month while ignored; concentration >20% revenue: valuation -20-30% and a churn event costs the quarter; discounting 20-40% to close first logos locks low ACV into renewals; SMB neglect: self-serve growth drops 30-50% |
| Asymmetry | doing everything right still gives about 25-35% win on qualified enterprise opps and a 20-30% chance a given deal ends as no-decision |
