# Startup credit and perk programs, 2026

Research status: built from web search snippets and aggregator pages, not from fetched official terms. Nearly every program's amounts and eligibility disagree across sources, so every figure below is a planning range. Items marked [unverified] could not be confirmed against the provider's own page. Check the official page before any real application. Design numbers for the game are marked [design].

## Program summaries

**AWS Activate.** Two routes. *Founders* is self-serve, with a starting tier around $1,000 and reported ceilings up to $5,000 [unverified]. *Portfolio* is for pre-Series B startups applying through an Activate Provider (VC, accelerator or incubator) and is reported at up to $200,000 by AWS's own guide, with third-party guides saying $100,000 [unverified]. Typical duration is 12 to 24 months. Needs a paid AWS account, a live company website, and a non-parked domain. Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/applying-for-aws-activate-credits-a-step-by-step-guide ; https://cloudvisor.co/aws-activate-program/

**Google for Startups Cloud Program.** Standard Start tier is up to about $2,000 over one year; Scale tier is up to about $200,000 over two years, with coverage split by year (roughly 100% of costs up to $100k in year one, then 20% in year two) [unverified]. The AI-first tier is stated on Google's page as up to $350,000 over two years, plus up to $10,000 for partner LLMs in Model Garden. Eligibility: AI-first company, VC funding (seed to Series A), founded within 10 years, under $5,000 of prior Google Cloud credits, and it must use or plan to use Vertex AI or Gemini. Higher tiers are often routed through a partner investor or accelerator. Sources: https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai ; https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/google-for-startups-cloud

**Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub.** Open to ideation-stage founders with no funding. Entry credits reported around $1,000 (up to about $5,000 with verification) [unverified]. Headline maximum about $150,000 of Azure, usually gated by an investor-network referral. Reported expiry: initial credits 90 days, later credits 180 days, forfeited if unused [unverified; source is a community answer]. OpenAI access is described as Azure OpenAI consumed against Azure credits, not a separate OpenAI grant. Sources: https://www.infoworld.com/article/3653229/microsoft-opens-azure-based-startups-founders-hub-easing-eligibility-rules.html ; https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/microsoft-for-startups

**Anthropic (Claude for Startups).** Aggregators describe a direct-application tier from about $1k to $5k, a referred tier from $5k to $25k, and a top tier up to $100k for VC-nominated companies, with no public checklist [unverified]. Credits apply to first-party Claude API through the Console, not Bedrock or Vertex. Typical term is 6 to 12 months. An October 2026 expansion (US$1,000, six months) was reported by one Spanish-language outlet and not corroborated [unverified]. Source: https://guptadeepak.com/anthropic-claude-for-startups-the-complete-guide-to-credits-tiers-and-eligibility-2026/ ; check claude.com/programs/startups.

**OpenAI for Startups.** Credits in 2026 appear to be gated behind VC partners (named partner firms in one guide include Sequoia, a16z, Thrive, Kleiner Perkins). No broad self-serve grant was found [unverified]. Ramp offers reportedly unlock about $2,500 of OpenAI credits [unverified]. OpenAI Grove (five-week SF cohort) lists $50,000 in API credits, but Cohort 2 applications closed January 12, 2026. Source: https://fin.ai/learn/ai-credits-for-startups

**NVIDIA Inception.** Free membership, no equity. Benefits are preferred pricing on a subset of hardware and free Deep Learning Institute training. Cloud GPU credits are issued by partners (AWS Activate, Nebius AI Lift) with amounts set by them, so the "up to $100k" figure depends on qualification [unverified]. More than 40,000 members as of March 2026 per NVIDIA. Source: https://hub.causo.ai/how-to-apply-to-nvidia-inception-2026

**Cloudflare for Startups.** Largest reported figure, up to about $250,000 across the developer platform and some enterprise-tier protections [unverified]. Source: https://betterstack.com/community/comparisons/cloudflare-vs-vercel-startups.md

**Vercel.** Standard program up to about $30k credits; the AI Accelerator route is reported at $216k plus $8M of AI partner credits, from a secondary source [unverified].

**Supabase.** Time-limited rather than dollars: reportedly up to six months of Team plan free (about $3,600 value) for bootstrapped, pre-seed and seed startups [unverified]. It also co-sponsors Vercel's AI Accelerator.

**MongoDB for Startups, Twilio, Algolia.** No reliable 2026 terms found. Treat as [unverified] and do not model.

**Datadog.** No direct startup program found. A year of Datadog (up to $100k value) appears only as a Mercury banking perk [unverified].

**Sentry.** No startup credit program found. Open-source sponsorship plan only.

**PostHog.** Aggregators report a $50,000 credit pool for YC companies, renewing annually for companies under $25M raised, plus a large free tier. The two accounts conflict [unverified].

**Mixpanel.** Year on the Startup plan for companies under five years old with under $8M raised; data must flow within 90 days [unverified].

**Segment (Twilio).** Reported up to $50,000 of Team plan credits for companies under two years old and under $5M raised [unverified].

**HubSpot for Startups.** Discount, not credits. Reported 90% off year one for companies with partner affiliation or verified venture funding, stepping to 50% then 25%; a smaller 30% for bootstrapped startups [unverified]. Discounts usually apply to new Professional or Enterprise products only; Starter is excluded. Source: https://www.startupeditor.com/?p=14674

**Notion, Linear, Slack, Intercom Early Stage, Zendesk for Startups, Gusto and Deel discounts.** Not verified in this pass. Model as small, generic discounts rather than specific values [design].

**Stripe Atlas and perk marketplaces (Brex, Ramp, Mercury).** Atlas is a $500 incorporation service; perks are reported at $200k to $350k+ aggregate across partners, mostly cloud credits, $2.5k to $5k of AI credits and SaaS discounts [unverified]. Stripe's support page lists separate cash-back offers for deposits above $75k, and at least one is limited to C-corps. Credits from different marketplaces can be the same underlying allocation. Sources: https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-atlas-perks-partners ; https://agentdeals.dev/startup-credits

**Accelerator bundles (YC, Techstars) and aggregators.** Accelerators often bundle partner credits, but no official YC page was checked. Aggregators (JoinSecret, Deepak's directory, Superscout, Agent Deals) list programs and figures that are secondary and frequently outdated.

## Realistic captured value

Nominal headline totals ($100k to $500k) are common in marketing. Most founders who are not in an accelerator or VC-referred path capture **$5k to $30k** in usable credits [design, based on the tier structure above]. Founders with VC referrals to AWS Portfolio or Google AI or Azure top tiers can capture **$100k to $350k** nominal, but usually spread over one to two years and often only a fraction used on the right services [design]. Approval typically takes days to a few weeks for self-serve tiers and several weeks to months for partner-gated ones [design, [unverified]].

## Behavioural effects (game-relevant)

- **Over-provisioning.** Credits make founders pick larger instances, managed services and multi-region setups early. [design] Model as a cost to later cleanup.
- **Lock-in.** Credits pull teams into one provider's services (Bedrock, Vertex, Azure OpenAI), which raises switching cost later. [design]
- **Credit cliff.** Credits either expire unused (low-usage startups) or run out just as usage grows (AI inference, data pipelines). Once the balance hits zero, the next charge appears on the bill with no warning. [design, one guide source]
- **Excluded services.** Marketplace purchases, some GPU instance families, third-party SaaS and sometimes support plans are excluded from credit coverage. Check each offer's exclusions.
- **Eligibility traps.** Needs a live company domain, LinkedIn page, paid account, and no prior credits from the same program. Applying with a parked domain is a common denial.

## Accounting and tax (brief)

Credits are generally not income. One practitioner view treats a promotional cloud credit that offsets a bill as a price reduction, recorded as a contra-expense against the gross cost [unverified; Beancount guide]. Prepaid or unused credits may need deferral depending on cash versus accrual basis and contract terms. A founder should not count unused credits as cash or runway. Whether ASC 450 applies to credits was not established by any source found; it is probably the wrong framework for a prepayment or price reduction and should be confirmed with an accountant [unverified]. Tax treatment of credits and any deferred tax asset question were not sourced.
