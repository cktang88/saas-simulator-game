# Realism review: B2B sales, relationships, inbound and calendar

Reviewed 2026-10-14 (in-game year 2026). Scope: `apps/crm.js`, `apps/outreach.js`, `apps/calls.js`, `apps/sales/*`, `apps/success.js`, `apps/success/*`, `core/network.js`, `apps/messages.js`, `data/inbound-archetypes.js`, `core/calendar.js`, `apps/calendar.js`, `apps/mail.js`.
Method: I read the code and research docs, then ran headless Node simulations with offline fallbacks only, since no LLM key was set. All numbers below come from those runs. Small samples are noted where they matter. The simulation scripts are listed at the end.

## Summary

The top-of-funnel parameters are well sourced. With 1,600 prospects the game produces about 0.75 meetings per 100 prospects (research: 0.5–2.5), 74–77% inbox placement, a complaint rate of about 0.1% of inboxed mail, 25–30% no-shows, and win rates and cycle lengths in range for each ACV band once SOC 2 is in place. The weak points are elsewhere:

- **The player can retry outcomes for free.** Booking a follow-up re-rolls qualification. Re-asking for a meeting re-rolls acceptance. Inbound and intro outcomes resolve whatever the founder writes.
- **Buyer budget scales with the seller's price, and seat count has no cap.** Price never costs a deal, and a 500-seat order to an 8-person firm wins 16% of the time.
- **Customer success is far too generous once the player acts.** Renewals are accepted at 96%. QBRs open a +50% seat expansion every quarter, which gives survivors +90% seats per year.
- **Personas leak hidden facts.** They state an exact budget figure (derived from your price) on the first price question.
- **Some mechanics are too harsh or wrongly triggered.** AI SDR books 0 meetings in 4 months. Verified lists bounce at 4–5x the benchmark per prospect. A $1.5k-ACV "enterprise" deal gets a SOC 2 review 70% of the time. The inbound cap squeezes out valuable messages after a launch or raise.

## What IS realistic (keep)

- **Deliverability stack.** It has secondary domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC factors, a 5–10 → 30 warmup ramp, a 30/inbox safe cap, an Instantly-style auto-pause at ≥5% bounces after 200 sends, a complaint target and ceiling of 0.1% and 0.3%, and open tracking shown as unreliable. sim1: placement **74–77%**, complaints **0.05–0.10% of inboxed**, domain reputation 74–88 at about 30 sends per inbox per day.
- **Top-of-funnel output.** sim1 gives about **1.6% positive replies across tiers and 0.75 meetings per 100 prospects** after one ask (research 0.5–2.5). The researched tier gets 4.6–5.5% replies (research 5–10%) and the warm tier 8.9–10.8% (research 10–20%).
- **No-shows.** Lead-time bands, SMB/ent offsets, reminders and a ~50% rebook give 25–30% observed.
- **Deal math with SOC 2 in place.** A good discovery call qualifies 52–55% (research 50–60%). Win of qualified by ACV:
  - $588: 27–53% (seed noise; mean ≈ 37%)
  - $4.8k: 34–42%
  - $24k: 37%
  - $30k ent: 31%
  - $108k–150k: 21–26% (research 12–22% for $100k+, so slightly high)

  Median cycles: $588 11–16 d, $4.8k 28–32 d, $24k 63–76 d, $30k ent 73–88 d, $108k 99–136 d, $150k ent 124–139 d. These match research bands (0–25, 15–45, 60–90, 90–120, 90–180).
- **Calls app.** The Mom Test and MEDDICC scorecard, the talk-ratio coaching and "keep me posted is not a next step" are the right skills to teach. The LLM prompts tell the persona to be skeptical about unshipped features.
- **Founder no-show.** A founder no-show loses the deal 50% of the time (10 of 16 missed calls in sim1). Calendar overbooking leads to missed meetings.
- **Inbound mix and volume by stage.** Idea 4.4/wk, launched 13.4, traction 21.8, funded 140 expected. The valuable share is 19–24% → 12% when funded, which matches the research [design] bands of 3–8, 12–25, 20–40 and 50–120, with a valuable share of 15–25% → 5–15%. The archetype red flags (wrong round, calendar link, pay-to-play) are good triage practice.
- **Intro funnel defaults** (when not exploited): weak ties make 35% of intros and meetings follow 71% of the time. A no-blurb ask is ×0.5. Each repeated ask within 90 days costs −15% fatigue.
- **CS structure.** It covers T−90/60/30 renewal windows, procurement asks (SIG/CAIQ, VPAT, EU residency, localization) with realistic hours, an answer-library effect, champion turnover with "follow the champion", and a counter-offer anchored on a 15–20% discount.

## Out-of-area observations (for other reviewers)

- sim3 passive CS: **first-renewal logo retention 30–42%** (ent 31/80 at day 400). The drivers are helpdesk `felt_ignored` cancels (20% per unanswered ticket; 11–30 per 80 accounts), `payment_failed` (10 of 78 at the SMB annual renewal) and legal `contract_terms` (19/80 ent). Enterprise GRR should be about 90% (SaaS Capital 2025). Even "active" CS only reached 47.5% logos. The helpdesk and legal owners should check these rates.
- The default `ent` plan is $1,500/yr (`core/state.js` MODELS). That is not mid-market or enterprise ACV ($15k+). Combined with S16, every ent deal gets enterprise procurement friction at SMB prices.
